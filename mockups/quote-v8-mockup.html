AI Quote — v8 Quote Page Redesign

Goal: The current quote page tries to show everything at once — customer banner, parts row, machine setups, materials, DXF list, finishing services, pricing tiers, summary, BOM. Result: cognitive overload, can't focus on one part at a time.

Proposal: Customer at the top (expanded), parts shown as cards in a row, click a part to expand a per-part wizard that only shows the sections relevant to that part's type (machined / cut blank / welded / hybrid / purchased). Each wizard step is one card — go through them sequentially, never see fields you don't need.
